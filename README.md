# 🎬 ACTION

### *If not this, why that.*

**A press for films you can hold.**
Drop in a video. ACTION turns it into a **REELOID**: one small file that carries the film, its title card, a closing card, and its own little cinema.

**[▶ Open ACTION](https://ovidemetrian.github.io/ACTION/)** · works best on a phone

## Lights down

Most video today lives on someone else's platform. You send a link, and the film stays behind, in a feed, under their rules, until the day it doesn't.

A REELOID travels the other way. **You send the film itself.** The receiver taps it, the house lights dim, the curtains part, and the film plays. No app, no account, no server. When it ends, a card rises with your words and your signature, like the last line of a letter.

> *Lumină, nu vrăjală* — light, not trickery.

## The press

ACTION works top to bottom on one screen. Every step is short, and every step says what it does.

| Step | What it does |
|---|---|
| **INTAKE** | Drop a video or tap to choose one. MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio travels everywhere. |
| **TRIM** | Set the IN and OUT points, so only the moment that matters goes. |
| **THROW** | Choose how far the reel has to travel: **LONG** (20 MB), **MID** (40 MB), **SHORT** (100 MB), or **NONE**, a keeper copy. |
| **SHAPE** | Square only: **480 × 480** to send, **720 × 720** to keep. One shape to render, one shape to press. |
| **LEGEND** | A title card before the film: choose a picture, or go straight to the film. **SEALED** holds the title back until it plays. |
| **SOUVENIR** | The card shown when the film ends: up to 90 characters, optional links, and your signature as the last line. |
| **PRESS · SEND · SAVE** | Press the REELOID, send it through the phone's share sheet, or save it to the device. |

## The REELOID

What the receiver gets is a small theatre in a single file:

- 🎟️ **A title card** before the film, sealed if you want a surprise
- 🎭 **Theatre curtains** that open on the first frame
- 💡 **Wandering house lights** that dim as the film begins
- ✂️ **A punch-hole crop**: pinch, drag and double-tap to frame the shot
- 📜 **A closing card ceremony**: your words, your links if you chose to show them, your signature
- 📦 **THROW delivery**, sized for the journey it has to make

**Links are off by default.** Some souvenirs aren't advertisements.

---

## Why a single file

| A link | A REELOID |
|---|---|
| Lives on a platform | Lives with the person who received it |
| Needs an account, a server, a company | Needs a browser |
| Disappears when the service changes | Plays as long as HTML opens |
| Belongs to the feed | Belongs to the moment |
