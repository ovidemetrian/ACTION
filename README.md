## Two gifts

### 🍨 For a song: the **SONOID**

You know mixtapes? Musical greeting cards?

**This is one you text.** Their face on the front, your face at the end, and your song inside. It doesn't live in an app or behind a link. It *is* the song, so it never stops working.

…and that's called a **SONOID**. **SONOR** is the app on your phone that makes them.

### 🎬 For a video: the **REELOID**

You know how a video you send just sits there in the chat?

**This one arrives as its own little cinema.** The lights dim, the curtains open, your film plays, and it ends with a card from you.

…and that's called a **REELOID**. **ACTION** is the app on your phone that makes them.

## Why a gift, not a link

| A link | A sonoid or reeloid |
|---|---|
| Lives on a platform | Lives with the person who received it |
| Needs an app, an account, a company | Needs a phone that opens a web page |
| Disappears when the service changes | Keeps playing |
| Belongs to the feed | Belongs to the moment |

## 🍨 SONOR, in more detail

**The front door.** SONOR opens on one simple screen:

1. **Choose a song**, or **say something** to record your voice (up to 3 minutes).
2. Add **your picture and theirs**, **your name and theirs**, and a few words if you like.
3. Tap **Another look** to try a new style, shown live.
4. Tap **MAKE IT**, then **SEND IT**.

**Change the look, all twenty steps** opens the full press, already filled in: lettering, meters, backdrops, moving logos, a ticker, spatial sound, and more.

**It also makes a music video**, in square (1080 × 1080) or vertical (1080 × 1920, 9:16) MP4 that drops straight into mobile video editors. What you see in the preview is exactly what the video records.

**Sound that moves around the room.** SONOR can place a song in a 3D room, and the listener's phone rebuilds the room itself, so the music is never re-recorded.

## 🎬 ACTION, in more detail

**The show:**

👆 Tap the reeloid → 💡 house lights dim → 🎟️ title card → 🎭 curtains part → 🎬 the film → 📜 closing card → ▶ play again

| Step | What it does |
|---|---|
| **INTAKE** | Drop a video or tap to choose one. MP4 travels everywhere. |
| **TRIM** | Set the in and out points, so only the moment that matters goes. |
| **THROW** | Choose how far the reel has to travel: LONG (20 MB), MID (40 MB), SHORT (100 MB), or NONE for a keeper copy. |
| **SHAPE** | Square only: 480 × 480 to send, 720 × 720 to keep. |
| **LEGEND** | A title card before the film, or straight to the film. SEALED keeps the title secret until it plays. |
| **SOUVENIR** | The card at the end: up to 90 characters, optional links, and your signature. |

**Links are off by default.** Some souvenirs aren't advertisements.
